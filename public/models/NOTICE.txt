u2netp.onnx
-----------
U^2-Net (small version, "U2-Netp") salient object detection model.

  Xuebin Qin, Zichen Zhang, Chenyang Huang, Masood Dehghan, Osmar R. Zaiane
  and Martin Jagersand. "U^2-Net: Going Deeper with Nested U-Structure for
  Salient Object Detection." Pattern Recognition 106, 2020.

  Original weights: https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
